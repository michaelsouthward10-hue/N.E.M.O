About Mythose Library

Mythos is an archive dedicated to preserving ancient myths, legends, and folklore from civilizations across the globe.

We aim to make ancient storytelling accessible, engaging, and beautifully organized for readers everywhere. Every account free or paid will be able to submit a story or tale that was or still is part of their life or culture, possibly passed down from older generations or something your town has implemented into it's history. We want everyone's story and tale to be eternalised. What's yours?
