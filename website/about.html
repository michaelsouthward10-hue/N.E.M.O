About Mythos Library

Mythos is an archive dedicated to preserving ancient myths, legends, and folklore from civilizations across the globe.

Every Story Eternalized

We aim to make ancient storytelling accessible, engaging, and beautifully organized for readers everywhere. Every account will be able to submit a story or tale that was or still is part of their life or culture.
